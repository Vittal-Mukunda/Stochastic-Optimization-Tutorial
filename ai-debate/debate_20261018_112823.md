## Opening solution

**Step 1: Reduce to residues mod 7.** Since n² + n + 1 depends only on n mod 7, test all seven residues:

| n mod 7 | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| n²+n+1 | 1 | 3 | 7 | 13 | 21 | 31 | 43 |
| mod 7 | 1 | 3 | **0** | 6 | **0** | 3 | 1 |

So the condition holds exactly when n ≡ 2 or 4 (mod 7).

(Sanity note: 2 and 4 are the primitive cube roots of unity mod 7 — 2³ = 8 ≡ 1, 4³ = 64 ≡ 1 — as expected since n²+n+1 = (n³−1)/(n−1).)

**Step 2: Count.** 1000 = 7·142 + 6, so residues 1,2,3,4,5,6 each appear in the final partial block (995≡1, 996≡2, 997≡3, 998≡4, 999≡5, 1000≡6).

- n ≡ 2: 2, 9, …, 996 → 996 = 2 + 7·142 → 143 values
- n ≡ 4: 4, 11, …, 998 → 998 = 4 + 7·142 → 143 values

Total = 143 + 143 = **286**.

**Numeric check (small case).** For n ≤ 14 the formula predicts 2 per block of 7, i.e. 4 values: n = 2 (7 = 7·1 ✓), 4 (21 = 7·3 ✓), 9 (91 = 7·13 ✓), 11 (133 = 7·19 ✓). Also verify a non-solution: n = 3 gives 13, not divisible. Matches.

**Check of the count.** Density 2/7 gives 1000·2/7 ≈ 285.7, consistent with 286. Also, n ≤ 994 (exactly 142 blocks) would give 284, and 995–1000 adds exactly 996 and 998 → 286. ✓

FINAL ANSWER: 286
