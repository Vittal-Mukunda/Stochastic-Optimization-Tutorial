### 7. Upper bounds on E_tail (what the crude counting gives)
- Per heavy T-arc P = v->w with x = 2 (all other pairs x <= 1): triples with t = 1 are either cyclic (at most cyc(P) = 12 of them) or transitive with c in V0 and both M-arcs into v, w carrying x = 1. Those c all satisfy x(c,v) = 1, so their number is at most gamma_v = (x-in-mass of v from V0) <= 13. Hence #(t=1 triples through P) <= 12 + 13 = 25, and the contribution of P is at most 50.
- Multi-heavy triples: a triple containing two heavy arcs must have three V1 vertices (two T-arcs sharing a vertex) or a V0 vertex with two heavy M-arcs. Each such triple has e <= 8 (here t <= 2). There are at most 6 pairs of heavy arcs in a triple for four heavy arcs, so at most 48 extra.
- Per heavy M-arc with x = 2: cyc = 11 triples through it. Those with c in V0 are L = 14 (e = 2 each). Those with c in V1 need x(v,c) = 1. Transitive triples through it with c in V0 (c top or middle, both needing c->v) have t = alpha = x(c,v), so they contribute only if x(c,v) = 1; there are at most gamma_v <= 13 such c. So a crude bound per M-heavy arc is 2 * (11 + 13) = 48.
- Consequence: the crude bounds (about 50 per heavy arc, four arcs about 200) exceed every requirement in the table (20 to 66). No K = 4 configuration is killed by these counts.
