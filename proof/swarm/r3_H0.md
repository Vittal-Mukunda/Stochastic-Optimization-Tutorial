## Log

### 0. Notation check
The brief says Q_y = "the vertices of R that y beats". That conflicts with AUDIT-52-B sec 1 and r2_CASEB, which use Q_y = R cap N^-(y) (the vertices of R that beat y), and M_y = R minus Q_y (the vertices of R that y beats). I use the second convention. In h = 0: |Q_y| = 5, |M_y| = 6.

### 1. Setup of h = 0, re-derived
- P = {a,b}, R = D(P) with |R| = 11, T[R] 5-regular, O with |O| = 39. For u in R: d^-(u) = 25 + j_u, with 5 in-neighbours in R and 20 + j_u in O (no in-neighbour in P). Sum j_u = 14.
- Each y in O has q_y = 5 (h = 0), so y beats exactly M_y (6 vertices of R) and is beaten by Q_y (5 vertices of R).
- Check: sum over y of |M_y| = 234 = sum over u of (20 + j_u) = 220 + 14 = 234. OK.
- Out-sets O_w = N^+_R(w) for w in R: 11 five-sets. Each point u lies in exactly 5 of them.
- Family F = {O_w} (11 five-sets) plus {M_y} (39 six-sets). Each point u has degree 5 + 20 + j_u = 25 + j_u. OK.
- For a triple Y in R: k(Y) = x_Y + m_Y, where x_Y = #{w : Y in O_w} and m_Y = #{y : Y in M_y}. F1 says k(Y) >= 5.
- Totals: sum over Y of k = 11 * C(5,3) + 39 * C(6,3) = 110 + 780 = 890. Since 165 * 5 = 825, the total excess is sum e_Y = 65. OK.
- Pairs: D_uv = c_uv + m_uv, with c_uv = #{w : u,v in O_w} (common in-neighbours in R), m_uv = #{y : u,v in M_y}. Sum over the 55 pairs of D = 11 * 10 + 39 * 15 = 695, so sum of delta_uv = 35 (delta = D - 12). OK.
- Sum over v of delta_uv = 5 j_u (from sum_v D_uv = 120 + 5 j_u). OK.
- Pair-excess identity: s(u,v) := sum over w of e_uvw = 4 D_uv - c_uv - 45 = 3 + 4 delta_uv - c_uv >= 0, with c <= 4 and D >= 12 (AUDIT-52-B 2(b)). OK.

### 2. Link lemma (any T[R])
For u in R, let the link H_u be the family on R minus u with 5 four-sets O_w minus u (w beats u) and 20 + j_u five-sets M_y minus u (y beats u). For v != v' in R minus u, the number of link sets containing {v,v'} equals k({u,v,v'}). So H_u covers every pair at least 5 times. The total pair coverage is 5 * 6 + (20 + j_u) * 10 = 230 + 10 j_u = 225 + Ex(u), where Ex(u) = 5 + 10 j_u. OK.

### 3. Biplane lemma (QR_11 case)
In QR_11 (Paley), c_uv = 2 for every pair (c = (p-3)/4 = 2). The out-sets O_w form a symmetric 2-(11,5,2) design (a biplane): k(k-1) = 20 = lambda(v-1) = 2 * 10. In a symmetric design any two distinct blocks meet in exactly lambda = 2 points.
- So no triple lies in two out-sets. Check by the identity sum over Y of C(x_Y,2) = sum over pairs of blocks of C(|O cap O'|,3) = 0 (since C(2,3) = 0).
- Hence x_Y in {0,1}. Sum x_Y = 110 gives 110 triples with x = 1 and 55 with x = 0. Check: 110 + 55 = 165. OK.
- Requirement: m_Y >= 5 for the 55 triples with x = 0, and m_Y >= 4 for the 110 triples with x = 1. Total requirement 275 + 440 = 715, and sum m_Y = 780, excess 65. OK.
- Consequence for L6: the table n_3 = x in {0,1,2} can be restricted to x in {0,1}. The "n_0 = 2 - x" count is right.

### 4. Correction of r2_CASEB L6(d)
L6(d) takes the 39 blocks to contain the 11 complements C_w = R minus O_w (6-sets), so that the other 28 blocks must 3-cover all triples. That needs C_w to be some M_y. Nothing in the setup gives this: C_w = {w} plus the in-neighbours of w in R, while M_y is the set of R-vertices beaten by y. So L6(d) is a special case, not the general problem. (Under that extra assumption, the requirement on the 28 residual blocks is exactly 3 for every triple, since 5 - x - (2 - x) = 3.)

General QR problem (no extra assumption): find 39 six-subsets M_y of an 11-set R such that
(a) each point u lies in exactly 20 + j_u of them, with j_u >= 0 and sum 14;
(b) every triple Y satisfies m_Y >= 5 - x_Y, with x_Y in {0,1} from the biplane (x_Y = 1 iff Y lies in some out-set);
(c) every pair satisfies m_uv >= 10 (from D >= 12 with c = 2). Source: AUDIT-52-B sec 2(b), which uses only (S) and c <= 4. It is a consequence of the setup, not of (b).

### 5. V_0 link lemma (QR_11, vertex u with j_u = 0)
- Since sum over v of delta_uv = 0 and delta >= 0, every D_uv = 12 for v != u. So m_uv = 10 for all v.
- The link structure: let F_t = O_t minus u for each t in N^-(u). These are 5 four-subsets of X = R minus u, |X| = 10. Any two of them meet in exactly one point (O_t cap O_t' has 2 points, one of which is u).
- Each point v of X lies in c(u,v) = 2 of the F_t. So each point of X lies in exactly 2 of the 5 four-sets. A set system with 5 blocks of size 4, pairwise meeting in one point, and 10 points each in exactly 2 blocks: the blocks correspond to the vertices of K5, and the points correspond to the edges of K5 (each pair of blocks meets in exactly one point, and there are C(5,2) = 10 such pairs). So X = E(K5), and F_t = the star at vertex t.
- Pair types on X: adjacent (edges sharing a K5-vertex) has eps = 1. Disjoint has eps = 0. The eps = 0 graph is the Petersen graph.
- The 20 five-sets C = M_y minus u (y beats u) are 5-subsets of E(K5). Each edge lies in 10 of them. Pair coverage: lambda(e,e') = #{C containing e,e'} = 5 - eps(e,e') + f(e,e'), where f >= 0.
- The excess at each point is sum_{e'} f(e,e') = s(u,e) = 1 (since s = 3 + 4 delta - c = 3 - 2 = 1). So f is a perfect matching on E(K5). Total check: sum lambda = 200 = 195 + 5. OK.
- So the local condition at u is: a multiset of 20 five-subsets of E(K5), each edge in 10 of them, with lambda = 4 on adjacent pairs, lambda = 5 on disjoint pairs, except that a perfect matching of disjoint pairs has lambda = 6.

### 6. Explicit local solution (verified)
Let K5 have vertex set Z5. Let S_i = {i,i+1} (short) and L_i = {i,i+2} (long). Let A = {i : S_i in block} and C = {j : L_j in block}. Take the four representatives:
- X0: A = {1,2,3,4}, C = {2}.
- X1: A = {0,1}, C = {0,2,3}.
- X2: A = {0,2}, C = {2,3,4}.
- X3: A = {1,3}, C = {3,4,0}.
Take the 20 blocks to be the Z5-orbits of these 4 representatives. None of the four representatives is Z5-invariant (an invariant 5-set would be all short or all long edges, and A, C here have sizes 4, 2, 2, 2 and 1, 3, 3, 3), so each orbit has exactly 5 blocks.

Pair-type counts per representative (n_T = number of pairs of type T in the block):
- SS (S_i, S_{i+1}), i.e. i, i+1 in A: X0 3, X1 1, X2 0, X3 0. Total 4 = required 4 (adjacent).
- SS' (S_i, S_{i+2}): X0 3, X1 0, X2 1, X3 1. Total 5 = required 5 (disjoint).
- LL (L_j, L_{j+2}): X0 0, X1 2, X2 1, X3 1. Total 4 = required 4 (adjacent).
- LL' (L_j, L_{j+1}): X0 0, X1 1, X2 2, X3 2. Total 5 = required 5 (disjoint).
- SL with d = j - i in {0,1,3,4} (adjacent): each of these d-values gets total 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 4 = required 4.
- SL with d = 2 (disjoint, S_i vs L_{i+2}): X0 0, X1 2, X2 2, X3 2. Total 6 = 5 + f = 6, and these 5 pairs form a perfect matching S_i - L_{i+2}.
- Checks: per-block pair totals are 10 (X0: 3+3+0+0+4+0 = 10; X1: 1+0+2+1+6 = 10; X2: 0+1+1+2+6 = 10; X3: 0+1+1+2+6 = 10). OK.
- Edge counts: short edges per representative a = 4, 2, 2, 2 (total 10); long edges 1, 3, 3, 3 (total 10). Each edge lies in 10 blocks. OK.
- Also each K5-vertex x: sum over blocks of (deg_B(x) - 2)^2 = 8 + 2 f_x, which holds here with f_x = 0 (the matching is all disjoint pairs).

So the local V_0 condition at a QR vertex of in-degree 25 has a consistent solution. No contradiction comes from one link.

### 7. Dead ends and checks
- DE-A (spectral on the local link): N = A^T A = 5I + 5J - E + F (E = line graph of K5, which is the triangular graph T(5), 6-regular, eigenvalues 6, 1, -2; F = perfect matching). On 1-perp, 5I - E has eigenvalues 4 and 7, and F has eigenvalues in [-1,1], so N is positive definite there. No contradiction.
- DE-B (first moments): sum Ex = 195 = 3 * 65, sum_v s(u,v) = 10 + 20 j_u, sum of pair-excess = 35. All consistent. No contradiction from counting alone.
- DE-C (second moment over triples): sum over Y of m_Y^2 = 39 * 20 + sum over ordered pairs (Q,Q') of C(1 + |Q cap Q'|, 3), with Q = complement of M_y. This gives only a lower bound on the variance. No cap, so no contradiction.
- DE-D (V_0 may be empty): sum j = 14 on 11 points does not force a zero. So the V_0 link may not be available; the case with all j_u >= 1 needs a separate analysis (not done).
- DE-E (full S_4, not used): under full S_4 each 4-subset of R has a dominator. For a V_0 vertex u, each triple of X = E(K5) must lie in some F_t or some C. The F_t cover 20 triples and the C's must cover the other 100 triples of X. This is a further test of the local solution in section 6 that I did not carry out.

### 8. Verdict and surviving constraints
- h = 0: UNDECIDED. Nothing proved excludes it.
- QR_11 sub-case: the reduction in r2_CASEB L6 is a special case. The general reduction is the 39-block problem in section 4. The V_0 local link is satisfiable (section 6). The global problem is open.
- General T[R]: the link lemma (section 2) and the pair/triple counts are consistent. No identity found.
- Surviving constraints to carry forward: (i) m_uv = D_uv - c_uv with D_uv = 12 at V_0 vertices and sum over v of delta_uv = 5 j_u; (ii) triple requirement m_Y >= 5 - x_Y; (iii) at a V_0 vertex the link is a multi-set of 20 five-subsets of E(K5) with the pair pattern of section 5.
